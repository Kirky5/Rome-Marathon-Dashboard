How This Block Works

🏃 3–4 runs, each with a job

One long run, one quality session (threshold, intervals, or marathon pace), and one or two genuinely easy runs. Junk miles are cut; every run has a purpose.

🚴 Zwift is your aerobic multiplier

2–3 Zwift sessions a week add aerobic volume without the impact of running. Most of them should be Z2 (HR <130). Zwift races are fine once a fortnight in Phases 1–3, then stop from January.

🏊 Swim for recovery & technique

One easy 30–45 min swim a week, ideally the day after the long run. Drills plus relaxed aerobic sets. It keeps your stroke over winter and flushes your legs.

❤️ Keep easy runs easy

Your easy runs have tended to drift into Z3 (HR 137–141). For this block, easy means HR <130, even if that's 6:00+/km. Run about 80% of the week easy and 20% hard.

🌙 Bed before 11:30 PM

Your own data shows it: in bed before 11:30 PM, sleep score is 83+; after midnight, it's around 73. Long-run weekends and the peak weeks depend on it.

⛽ Train the gut

From December, every long run of 20 km or more is a fuelling rehearsal: 60–90 g carbs per hour using the gels you'll race with. A first marathon usually goes wrong at 32 km because of fuelling, not fitness.

The Course
Profile
8–31 m
Flat overall. Short rises late on, plus a drop and a short climb in the final kilometre.
Surface
Cobbles
Runners report about 6–13 km of sampietrini in total: around the start, by the Vatican and in the final stretch. Slippery when wet.
Mid-March weather
15–19°C
Typical highs (lows 3–13°C). Has ranged from rain and thunderstorms to around 21°C. Warmer than Ayrshire, so plan fluids accordingly.

🏛️ Sights on the route

Colosseum · Piazza Venezia & the Vittoriano · Circo Massimo · the Lungotevere · Castel Sant'Angelo · Via della Conciliazione & St Peter's. Enjoy them, but run your own pace.

⏱️ Cut-off & aid

In 2023 the limit was 6h30. Refreshment stations at km 5, 7.3, 9.2, 13.4, 15, 17.7, 20.3, 21.2, 22.3, 25, 28.6, 30, 32.3, 35, 37.3, 40.2 and the finish. Electrolytes every ~5 km from km 5; sponges from 7.5 km. Check the 2027 Race Info PDF: these can change.

Pacing Plan

Start about 5–8 s/km slower than goal pace for the first 10 km. Corrals are crowded and the early cobbles cost energy. Settle at goal pace from 10 to 30 km, hold it from 30 to 40, then empty the tank. Run to effort and HR on the cobbles, not the watch.

Fuelling Plan

⛽ In-race (target 60–80 g carbs/hr)

WhenWhat
Take each gel just before a water station so you can wash it down. Practise this exact pattern on every long run of 20 km or more from Week 10. A 40 g gel (e.g. a "double" gel) at each point gets you to about 75 g/hr. A standard 22–25 g gel gives about 45–50 g/hr, so top up with the course electrolyte drink.

Kit Checklist

👟 Shoes

Race shoes broken in with 2 or more long runs (including the W19 32 km). Good grip matters on wet cobbles. Avoid a brand-new super shoe on race day.

🎽 Clothing

Race-tested shorts and vest, anti-chafe (feet, thighs, chest), cap for sun or rain, throwaway layer for the corral. Pack a light rain shell for the bag.

⌚ Tech & fuel

Garmin charged, with a marathon workout loaded (set auto-lap to 5 km so cobble GPS drift matters less). Gels in a belt or shorts. Bib, pins and printed race documents.
